# TSM (Taiwan Semiconductor ADR) — Risk Report

**Moderate risk · 5.5/10**

NYSE-listed depositary share · Five TSMC ordinary shares per ADS

## Summary

TSM is TSMC’s sponsored American depositary share traded on the NYSE. Each ADS represents five ordinary shares under the deposit agreement. Citibank is the depositary and its Taipei branch is the custodian. This equity/depositary exposure differs from the Robinhood Series 43 debt token that references the ADR.

## Volatility — 4.0

TSMC equity carries semiconductor-cycle, valuation and geopolitical risk. Concentrated leading-edge manufacturing in Taiwan creates a discrete disruption exposure that a trailing price range cannot measure fully. Neither the ADR nor the underlying ordinary share has a fixed redemption value.

## Liquidity — 9.0

The ADR trades on the NYSE with listed-market liquidity. Market depth, broker access and trading hours govern practical exits. The retained September 6, 2026 market observations are not current sized execution evidence, and ADR liquidity does not transfer automatically to the Robinhood token market.

## Counterparty — 6.5

The 2025 Form 20-F identifies Citibank as depositary and its Taipei branch as custodian, with five ordinary shares represented by each ADS. The depositary structure interposes contractual, custody and cross-border settlement risks between an ADS holder and the ordinary shares. Beneficial owners holding through a broker act through that relationship; voting rights depend on notice, instructions and the deposit agreement.

## Redemption — 6.5

The deposit agreement provides cancellation and delivery of underlying shares subject to fees, procedures and restrictions. This is securities delivery rather than a dollar payout promise. For non-Taiwan holders, cancellation can require a local agent, custodian and tax guarantor, alongside fees, taxes and eligibility. Taiwanese settlement, broker arrangements and effective amendments matter to access. A Deloitte corporate financial/ICFR audit does not assure collateral of Robinhood’s separate debt token.
