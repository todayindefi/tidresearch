# MSTR and raw STRC shared-source definitions

## Field map

| Evidence | Canonical source and fields | Report treatment |
|---|---|---|
| General cash and service reserve | `strategy_events.json`: USD_RESERVE_UPDATE.extracted.usd_cash_usd / reserve_usd; base `digital_credit_framework.usd_reserve_policy` | Discrete filing trend, preserving both meanings and filing dates; no daily interpolation or misleading average of repeated stale disclosures. |
| Repurchases | Events SECURITY_REPURCHASE.extracted.per_security_aggregate_price_usd.STRC and period_start/end | Repeated-period support and cash use. Authorization is not cash; execution average is not a holder quote. Period lengths differ. |
| BTC holdings | Base `tradfi.strategy_btc_holdings.count`, count_as_of/count_source | Use issuer-anchored count, retain scrape as a cross-check; market value changes are distinct from BTC transactions. |
| mNAV | Base `tradfi.mnav`, version v2; history `series[].mnav`, explicit mnav_definition_version=v2 | Same EV/BTC convention. Do not substitute common-market-cap/BTC, gross BTC per share, or net equity per share. Historical means exclude unversioned rows. |
| STRC market price and par | Base `tradfi.strc_secondary`; history `series[].strc_price` | Same Yahoo/yfinance source and $100 reference. Keep closed-market/stale-quote caveats. Describe observed price behavior, not a redemption guarantee. |
| MSTR market price / BTC market price | Base `tradfi.mstr` / `tradfi.btc`; yfinance / CoinGecko | Preserve producer choices and timestamps. No independently acquired replacement quotes. |
| Rate and cadence | Base `tradfi.strc_dividend.current_rate`, dividend_frequency, pending_terms, current_rate_source | Actual current terms separate from conditional proposals. Do not double-count daily and replaced semi-monthly dividends. |
| Support policy | Base `digital_credit_framework.strc_dividend_policy` and dcs_repurchase_program | Discretionary policy, not an automatic ratchet, segregated collateral or contractual price floor. |
| Service/runway | Base `cash_service_waterfall`, `tradfi.strc_dividend.outstanding_derivation`, risk_flags | Producer's model, with aged-base warning. No alternative forecast; do not endorse precise absolute runway while the base remains aged. |
| Raw STRC exit | Nasdaq venue/access; no order-book depth ladder in the shared feed | Liquidity 7.0 is a venue/access judgement, not a measured dollar threshold. Never import the STRCx DEX ladder or measure execution shortfall from $100 par. |

## Time horizon

The reports use 7/30/90-day arithmetic means of retained source observations and their observed ranges. Repeated closed-market quotes remain in those samples: they are not independent trades, exchange closing series or time-weighted statistics. Observation counts and maximum gaps are disclosed. Compatible mNAV rows carry v2 explicitly. Source-period financial facts are reviewed as filings rather than repeated once per monitor poll.

Strong recent prices and weak cash funding can coexist. Recovery toward par is credited on price behavior while issuer support is judged on repeat funding and depletion. Bad debt, a missed payment or a legal change can warrant immediate review without waiting for an averaging period. A favorable spot mark alone does not establish durable restoration.
